SecondPlate aims to reduce unnecessary food waste while protecting our planet and promoting sustainability. Our platform connects surplus food providers with verified NGOs, transforming unused meals into meaningful support. Together, we can build a smarter, greener, and more responsible food system where every rescued meal brings us closer to a hunger-free future.

By making food accessibility a shared responsibility, we empower vulnerable families and support communities in need.

Questions people ask

Is the food safety check reliable?

It gives a visual freshness estimate only. Always smell and check the food, and when in doubt, throw it out.

Which technologies power the project?

Python, TensorFlow or PyTorch with OpenCV for vision, a Flask or Django backend, a React front end and Firebase or MySQL for data.

Is it free to use?

Recipe suggestions and donations are free for households and NGOs.
